2. What we collect and why

  • Access and agreement. When you enter, we set cookies recording that you entered the access code, accepted the Terms and confirmed you are {{ minimum_age }}+. Purpose: let you in and remember your agreement. Basis: our contract with you.
  • Account (optional). Email address, a password (sent to our account provider, which stores only a secure hash — we never store it), and a nickname. Purpose: sign you in and keep saves private to you. Basis: contract.
  • Workspace saves (optional). Only if you choose to save to your account: your paper journals, watchlists and game progress. Broker keys, passwords and session cookies are never included. Basis: contract / your request.
  • Published game scores (optional). If you publish a Loki Dive score: your nickname, best score, number of publications and time — these are shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Chat room. The name you type and your messages and Loki's replies are stored on the server for up to 30 days (and at most the latest 400 turns across the room) so Loki can use recent context. Your own copy stays in your browser until you clear it. Do not type personal or sensitive information into chats.
  • Broker connections (optional). Alpaca paper keys are kept in your browser and sent to our relay only for the request you make; we do not store them. Tradovate credentials are used once to obtain an access token and are not stored; the token is kept only in an encrypted cookie for up to one hour.
  • Security records. To stop password guessing and abuse we briefly keep counters keyed by your IP address in server memory (cleared within about an hour or on restart). Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, page) under its own retention. Basis: legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

We do not collect payment details, government IDs, precise location, contacts, biometrics, or your date of birth, and we do not make automated decisions with legal effects about you.

3. Who we share it with

  • Hosting (Vercel) runs the website and keeps request logs.
  • Account and database provider (Supabase) stores accounts, optional saves and published scores.
  • Brokers you connect (Alpaca, Tradovate) receive your requests under their own privacy policies, only when you use those features.

Market data (prices, headlines) is fetched by our server from public providers without any of your personal data. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We disclose data if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Access/agreement cookies: 30 days. Sign-in cookie: 14 days. Chat cookie: 14 days. Tradovate cookie: 1 hour.
  • Chat turns on the server: 30 days at most.
  • Account, saves and published scores: until you delete them or your account.
  • Security counters: about an hour. Hosting logs: per the hosting provider's retention.
