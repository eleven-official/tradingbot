Loki uses only strictly necessary cookies and on-device storage that make features you ask for work. Blocking these cookies will stop the related feature (for example, entering the site or staying signed in).

Cookies

  • loki_gate — remembers you entered the access code. 30 days. Strictly necessary.
  • loki_terms — records which version of the Terms you accepted (including your 18+ confirmation). 30 days. Strictly necessary.
  • loki_session — keeps you signed in to your account. 14 days. Strictly necessary (only if you sign in).
  • loki_chat — keeps your chat-room name and session. 14 days. Strictly necessary for the chat room.
  • loki_tradovate — encrypted Tradovate access token. Up to 1 hour. Only if you connect Tradovate.
  • loki_broker_session — local-launcher broker link. Only on the desktop launcher, never on the website.

All are first-party, signed or encrypted, HttpOnly (not readable by page scripts), and sent only to this site.

Storage in your browser

Features you use keep their data in your browser's local storage, on your device only: paper trading journals (single-symbol AI, Portfolio AI, paper portfolio), watchlists, price alerts and Live-markets preferences, chat history, game progress, display currency, workspace checklist, and — if you enter them — your Alpaca paper keys. A once-per-visit flag in session storage stops the intro animation repeating. Nothing in local storage is sent to us unless you choose to save to your account.

Clear it any time with Clear Loki data from this browser, or through your browser settings.

Changes

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting it.
