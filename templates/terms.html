1. What Loki is

Loki is a self-service research and paper-trading workspace. Everything inside runs in strict paper mode on this site: simulated orders, simulated accounts, no execution of real trades through this website, and no movement of your real money.

Loki is free to use — no subscription and no paywall. The one thing the house asks for is its fee: 30% of recorded profits (§5 below, and the model explained on /revenue). Nothing is charged automatically, and no visitor's paper results or game points are ever treated as money.

No profits are promised. Nothing on Loki is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing here is a promise, forecast, or guarantee that you will make money. Markets are uncertain, most trading attempts lose money, and any idea, scan, backtest, score, or AI commentary shown here can be wrong, incomplete, late, or unavailable. If you use these tools and do not make money — or lose money acting on something you chose to act on elsewhere, under your own credentials, your own judgement remains yours — that outcome is yours to own, not Loki's.

3. Your account and your data

Your email and authentication credentials are handled by the account service. Workspace journals and game progress stay in your browser unless you explicitly save them to your account in the project database. Cloud workspace copies are private to your signed-in account. Publishing a game score makes your nickname, best score, publication count and publication time public; your email and private journals are not included. Account-specific browser records remain on the device and can be inspected by someone with access to that browser profile. Broker keys, passwords and session cookies are excluded from cloud workspace saves. The books behind the fee model (see /revenue) are private operator records.
