          AUTOPILOT · PAPER · TAB-OPEN ONLY

          Let Loki drive, watched.

          The whole rule in plain English: while this tab is open and the switch is ON, every 60 seconds Loki reads this ticker's last 25 one-minute bars from Alpaca's data feed (through the relay), averages the last ten closes, and — when the newest close crosses the average — buys exactly 1 share (market, day) crossing from below, or sells 1 crossing from below to above — but only if the autopilot itself bought that share; it never touches positions you opened by hand. Ten orders per session at most, one action in flight at a time, a 90-second cooldown after any send, and it pauses while the tab is hidden. Closing the tab or reloading turns it off — it never restarts on its own. This is paper money with handrails, not a strategy: every check and every decision is logged below, none hidden.
