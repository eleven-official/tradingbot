LLOKIREVENUE · HOW THE HOUSE IS PAID

PROFIT-SHARE, STATED PLAINLY

The house keeps 30% of recorded profits.

Loki bills a share of real, recorded profits — not of forecasts, not of paper gains, and never of anything a visitor plays with. This page explains the model. The figures themselves stay on the operator's side of the wall: money data answers only to the operator's key, so nothing about your use of Loki is posted here.

The fee model

30% OF RECORDED PROFITS
Computed server-side, per recorded event, with exact decimal math — {{ house_cut_bps }} basis points of the gross figure, rounded half-up to the cent. Losses and corrections are recorded the same way, and pull the books down.

What counts as “profit”: only an event an operator has actually recorded here, per month, per subject, with its provenance attached. Nothing is estimated, nothing is aggregated, nothing is backfilled from market moves.

What never happens automatically: nothing is auto-charged, no card is touched, no visitor's game points are money. This page is a ledger of a business arrangement, not a paywall.

The agreement itself (draft)

Fee: 30% of a subject's recorded monthly profit, invoiced monthly, computed by this ledger from the events the operator records. It is a service-side bookkeeping system; it does not execute payments yet — a later wave wires invoicing through a payment provider the operator owns.

Draft, not legal advice. The wording of an actual profit-share agreement — measurement, disputes, clawbacks, taxes — must be settled by the operator with a qualified professional before anyone signs anything or a cent moves.
