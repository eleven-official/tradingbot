loki remembers candles. Every time one of its setups worked out, the shape of the tape that made it happen joins a private scrapbook — body, wick, acceleration, and all. Then each bar closes, the current tape gets compared against that scrapbook, and the closest matches vote on what they did next. That's the whole engine. It's a memory trick, not magic, and it says so right in the HUD.

Built for human use. Paste it, pick a color theme, done. While it's warming up and the scrapbook is still thin, the HUD just tells you that instead of pretending to know anything. Nothing here repaints — once a bar closes, its call is final.

Say it plainly: the confidence number is a vote share — it means the closest memories lean one way, not that money shows up. It is not a TP/SL profit tracker. The Horizon hit-rate counts where price stood after the configured number of bars, on the most recent resolved signals, and flat closes lose — it is not a real trade P&L and costs are not subtracted. Audit it before trusting anything.

Field notes

  • 'VOTE POOL TIED' in the HUD is not a malfunction. It means neighbors couldn't settle a direction, so the signal gate stays shut until one side wins by the configured margin.
  • That M number on a badge follows the vote margin — the distance between the two neighbor pools at that call.
  • The script keeps quiet for a few bars right after an exit before it is allowed to signal again — a chill-off so it doesn't churn itself to death. Reversals sidestep the wait: they're active, not lazy.
  • Expectancy ×ATR is the average outcome of resolved signals, measured in average-bar-size units. Under zero over a long run and it's telling you to stop, not to adjust until it agrees with you.
  • Warm-up on a fresh chart takes some time to fill the memory. Don't trade on thin evidence; the HUD literally displays that condition.
  • Themes on the side panel: Midnight, Cyberpunk, Sunset, Aurora, or fully custom to your chart.

Chart checks

Use a standard intraday candlestick chart, with an S/R resolution that's a clean multiple of your chart (for example, 5-minute chart with a 15-minute level). Levels refresh only on the S/R bar close, and pivots also need the set right-side confirmation bars. If volume data is missing, the gate stays shut.
