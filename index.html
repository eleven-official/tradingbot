What each candle means:
Each candle represents a time period. The body shows the open-to-close move, and the wicks show the high and low extremes. Green = bullish close, red = bearish close.
Open = first price in the interval, Close = last price in the interval, High = highest price, Low = lowest price.
Use many candles together to see trend, momentum, and reversals. Longer bodies = stronger moves; long wicks = volatility and rejection.
